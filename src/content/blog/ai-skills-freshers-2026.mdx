The best advice for freshers in 2026 is simple: don't compete with AI, learn to work with it. Employers increasingly expect new hires to use AI tools fluently. Here are five skills that pay off across marketing, software and data roles.

## 1. Prompting that actually works

Knowing how to brief an AI tool — giving it context, examples and constraints — is now a core productivity skill. Good prompting is the difference between generic output and usable work.

## 2. AI-assisted content and research

Using tools like ChatGPT and Claude to draft, summarise and research — then editing critically. AI gives you a first draft in seconds; your judgement makes it good.

## 3. Reading data and dashboards

Whether it's GA4 for marketing or a Power BI report for operations, the ability to *interpret* numbers and decide what to do next is more valuable than ever.

## 4. Automating repetitive work

Simple automations — connecting apps, scheduling, cleaning data — free you to do higher-value work and signal that you think like a problem-solver.

## 5. Judgement: knowing when to trust AI

The most important skill is knowing when the AI is wrong. Understanding the fundamentals of your field lets you catch mistakes and make the final call.
