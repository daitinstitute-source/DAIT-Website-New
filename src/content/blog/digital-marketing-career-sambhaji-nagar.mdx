Digital marketing is one of the fastest ways to start an IT-adjacent career in Sambhaji Nagar — you don't need a coding background, and small businesses, agencies and startups across Maharashtra are hiring for it. Here's an honest look at what the path involves in 2026.

## Why digital marketing is a strong first career

Every business now needs to be found on Google, run ads, and build an audience on social media. That demand shows up as jobs: performance marketing, SEO, content, social media and analytics roles that a motivated fresher can grow into within months, not years.

## The skills employers actually want

- **Paid advertising** — running and optimising Google & Meta ad campaigns
- **SEO & content** — getting a website to rank, writing content with AI assistance
- **Analytics** — reading GA4 dashboards and reporting what worked
- **AI tools** — using ChatGPT, Canva AI and automation to do more, faster

The marketers who get hired can *show* work — a live campaign, a ranked blog post, a real dashboard — not just a certificate.

## Roles and realistic salaries

Entry roles like Digital Marketing Executive or Performance Marketing Associate typically start around ₹3–5 LPA in India, rising quickly with results and a portfolio. (Figures are market averages from sources like AmbitionBox and Glassdoor, not a guarantee.)
