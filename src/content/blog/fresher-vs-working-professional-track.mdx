The same subject — say digital marketing or software development — should be taught differently depending on where you are in your career. Here's how to decide.

## If you're a fresher

You need **foundations done well**: the fundamentals, a structured path from zero, and a portfolio you can take to interviews. Weekday, full-time batches let you go deep quickly, and placement support matters most here — resume, mock interviews and that crucial first project.

Look for: a Foundation track, beginner-friendly pacing, and real projects.

## If you're a working professional

You don't need the basics repeated — you need **depth and relevance**. The goal is to move from executor to strategist, or to switch into a better-paid role, without pausing your job. That means weekend or evening batches, and an advanced curriculum focused on the tools and workflows you'll use at work immediately.

Look for: an Advanced / Upskill track, flexible timings, and skills you can apply on Monday.
